# 3V3 half-court

- The ball handler uses the 1V1 shot timing, dribble moves, body contact and finishing. **E / gamepad B / touch 传球** passes while holding the ball and calls for the ball from a CPU teammate while off ball. A CPU evaluates the requester's separation, the passing lane and its own pass IQ; a declined call shows **暂不传球**. Pass accuracy affects delivery speed, and defenders with good pass perception can intercept.
- On offense without the ball, move near the ball handler's defender and press **F / gamepad R3 (press the right stick) / touch 掩护** to plant a screen. After planting, move to roll or pop yourself. CPU teammates choose screens when their ratings and spacing make sense; finishers roll and shooters pop. The ball handler looks for a clean pass to the open roller or popper. Screens use shared body collision plus a short movement penalty; defenders with better lateral defense and help IQ fight through more quickly.
- At check-ball and on possession changes, 3V3 matches guards with guards, wings with wings, and bigs (power forwards and centers) with bigs. Exact positions are preferred within those groups; distance breaks ties. The lobby, picker and in-game player cards show positions. CPU teammates can temporarily switch or protect the rim first on a drive, then return to their positional assignments. Help IQ affects the response.
- Off-ball shooters spot up in the corners, slashers make timed cuts, and CPU teammates clear to separate weak-side spots for isolation. Ratings are read per star from `r5.js`, with defaults for unlisted players. 3V3 does not inherit full-court transitions or 5V5 shot bonuses.
- After a make the other team checks the ball outside the arc at the top; that check satisfies the clearance requirement. Live-ball changes inside the arc require clearing before a shot can count. 3V3 ends at the selected target, without a two-point lead requirement.
- For online play, select the same mode in both browsers. One player creates a room and shares the code; the other joins and waits for the host to start. In 3V3, joins take open waiting slots, and any empty ones are filled by CPUs on start. The host simulates the match and guests send inputs over PeerJS/WebRTC; connectivity depends on the public signaling service and the players’ networks.

Run `node qa/3v3.cjs` for headless rules, passing and shot-clock checks, an end-to-end CPU match, and screen, rotation, off-ball and call-for-ball scenarios.
